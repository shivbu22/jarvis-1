# Executive Business Intelligence and Analytics

Real-time telemetry ingestion, anomaly detection, competitor moves monitoring, and market research synthesis across ventures.

```
[ Real-Time Data Ingestion Streams ]
  ├── User Traffic & Hourly Research Peaks (e.g. Medimin 2-3 PM peak)
  ├── Transactional Revenue & Affiliate Click-Throughs
  └── Operational Fleet & Queue Telemetry
              │
              ▼
[ Automated Metric Anomaly Detection ]
  ├── Flags sudden CAC spikes or churn deviations
  └── Alerts on API SLA degradations (< 99.5% uptime)
              │
              ▼
[ Competitive Reconnaissance & Market Gaps ]
  ├── Scrapes competitor pricing and feature rollouts
  └── Identifies unserved queries (e.g. missing generic substitutes)
              │
              ▼
[ Executive Morning Action Digest ]
```
