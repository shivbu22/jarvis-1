# Product Growth, Feature Prioritization, and Viral Loops

Framework for optimizing customer acquisition channels, engineering viral compounding loops, and prioritizing product backlogs.

## 1. Feature Backlog Prioritization (RICE Framework)

$$\text{RICE Score} = \frac{\text{Reach} \times \text{Impact} \times \text{Confidence}}{\text{Effort}}$$

- Categorizes candidate features into **Quick Wins** (high impact, low effort e.g. Pharmacy locations for Medimin), **Strategic Bets** (Doctor recommendations), and **Deprioritized Noise**.

## 2. Viral Loop Engineering & $K$-Factor

$$K = i \times c$$
*where $i$ is number of invites sent per customer, and $c$ is conversion rate per invite.*

- **Example (Quick Queue Referral Engine):** Every 5 referrals = ₹100 credit. Drives $K = 1.3$, yielding compounding organic growth with zero paid ad spend.
- **Budget Reallocation Rule:** Pour 60%+ of acquisition spend into channels where CAC is $\le 50\%$ of paid ads.
