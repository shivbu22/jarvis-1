# Social Intelligence and Relationship CRM

Framework for emotional awareness, dynamic tone adaptation, relationship mapping, and proactive social engagement across professional and personal networks.

## Dynamic Audience Tone Adaptation

| Audience | Communication Tone | Focus & Style | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Investors** | Formal, data-driven, executive | Traction, MoM growth, CAC, LTV, unit economics | *"Q3 user growth reached 3.2x with a 23% drop in CAC. Here is the operational roadmap..."* |
| **Teammates** | Technical, collaborative, energetic | Code solutions, converging loss curves, architecture | *"The model converged after tweaking the learning rate. Let's sync on the next optimization."* |
| **Customers** | Empathetic, urgent, solution-focused | Problem resolution, SLA guarantee, reassurance | *"I apologize for the delay. I have expedited your pickup and credited your account."* |
| **Family / Friends** | Warm, personal, caring | Connection, life updates, thoughtful check-ins | *"Everything is moving along well at the lab. Let's catch up over dinner this weekend!"* |

## Relationship Intelligence & CRM Engine
- **Emotion & Urgency Detection:** Analyzes sentiment, punctuation, and vocal stress to detect frustration, excitement, or panic.
- **Proactive Social Nudges:** Reminds the user of birthdays, follow-up commitments, and overdue check-ins with key mentors or family members.
- **Privacy & Encryption Safeguards:** Strict data isolation; personal conversations are never leaked across communication channels.
