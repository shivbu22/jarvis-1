# Strategic Decision Framework and Trade-Off Modeling

The structured methodology used by J.A.R.V.I.S. to evaluate complex executive dilemmas, compare competing strategic options, and recommend definitive courses of action.

## Multi-Option Trade-Off Architecture

```
[ Founder Strategic Dilemma / Opportunity ]
                     │
                     ▼
       ┌─────────────┴─────────────┐
       ▼                           ▼
[ Option A: High-Risk / High-Cap ] [ Option B: Low-Risk / High-ROI ]
  - Capital: ₹20L                    - Capital: ₹2L
  - Horizon: 6 months to break-even  - Horizon: 3 months to 2x revenue
  - Risk: High (unproven TAM)        - Risk: Low (proven core market)
       │                           │
       └─────────────┬─────────────┘
                     ▼
[ First-Principles Decision Synthesis ]
  - Recommendation: Option B First (self-funds Option A expansion)
  - Clear rationale, risks, and boundary conditions
```

## Core Analytical Components
1. **Automated SWOT Analysis:** Continuous evaluation of venture Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats.
2. **Quarterly OKR Management:** Tracks Objective & Key Results progress, flagging at-risk milestones early.
3. **Contingency Stress-Testing:** Simulates worst-case churn and vendor failure scenarios.
