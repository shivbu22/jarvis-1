# Venture Financial Optimization and Runway Forecasting

Financial modeling, burn rate tracking, cash runway projection, and investor relations pipeline management for seed/early-stage ventures.

## Financial Governance Framework

1. **Cash Flow & Runway Modeling:**
   $$\text{Runway (Months)} = \frac{\text{Total Available Cash Balance}}{\text{Net Monthly Burn Rate}}$$
   - Auto-triggers critical alerts when runway drops below 6 months.

2. **Unit Economics & Margin Optimization:**
   - Tracks Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Lifetime Value (LTV), and LTV:CAC ratios per acquisition channel.
   - Channel CAC thresholds: Referral (₹200) $\ll$ Direct (₹300) $<$ Paid Ads (₹450).

3. **Fundraising & Investor Pipeline Management:**
   - Tracks investor outreach stages (Initial Outreach $\rightarrow$ Partner Meeting $\rightarrow$ Term Sheet $\rightarrow$ Close).
   - Generates executive metrics packs (MoM growth, retention cohorts, gross margins).
