# Venture Medimin (Medicine Comparison)

Medimin is an AI-powered pharmaceutical analysis, generic medicine substitution, and clinical comparison engine.

## Key Technical Systems & Architectures

1. **Pharmaceutical NLP & Entity Extraction:**
   - Named Entity Recognition (NER) for active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), dosages, delivery mechanisms, and therapeutic classes.
   - Clinical text parsing from regulatory datasheets, packaging, and drug formularies.

2. **Chemical Composition Similarity & Equivalence:**
   - Exact molecular & salt-match algorithms for generic-to-branded equivalence.
   - Bioavailability index weighting and contraindication cross-matching.

3. **Recommendation & Pricing Optimization:**
   - Cost-per-unit therapeutic efficiency ranking.
   - Supply-chain availability feeds across Indian pharmacies and distributors.

4. **Data Quality & Validation Pipelines:**
   - Automated schema validation for drug databases.
   - Hallucination-free verification against approved pharmacopeia standards.
